District Intelligence & Public Relations Information System (DIPRIS)

DIPRIS is the central operational intelligence system for Transpower Electricity Distribution Limited (TEDL). It synchronizes district-level technical, commercial, customer, and regulatory data directly with corporate communication workflows. The platform ensures seamless accountability, rapid public crisis communication, unified district returns, and proactive stakeholder relations across all franchise territories.
